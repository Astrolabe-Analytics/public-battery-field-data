# Cao raw-pickle schema check

## What the authors say

Sources: Cao et al., *Nature Communications* 16, 1651 (2025), doi:10.1038/s41467-025-56832-8; Zenodo record 10656500 description text displayed 2026-09-10; and the held author-code v1.0.0 archive.

The paper and Zenodo record do not explain the three vehicle files in words. The released author code establishes that `vin_1` has shape `(rows, 1, 7)`: `Test_.py` line 28 feeds it to an LSTM loaded at line 27, and `Train_.py` sets `INPUT_SIZE = 7` near line 29. The paper names six model inputs, voltage, temperature, current, on-board SOC, speed, and mileage (p. 3, Fig. 2d); Fig. 2d calls them network input sample sequences. Measured `vin_1` values are in `[0, 1]` and feature 2 is always zero. It is therefore inferred to be six normalized inputs plus one unused slot, not raw telemetry.

`vin_2` has shape `(rows, 2 + N + N + 3)`. `Test_.py` lines 45-50 split it as `x` (2), `y` (`N = 110` for QAS), `z` (`N`), and `q` (3); `Train_.py` lines 51-153 feed `y` to the first autoencoder conditioned on `x` and `q`. What the blocks are is set out under Block meanings below.

`vin_3` has shape `(rows, 2 + N + N + 4)`. `Test_.py` lines 58-63 split it with a four-wide `q` and feed `y` to the second autoencoder. It is not a rescaled copy of `vin_2` but its state-of-charge counterpart, see Block meanings below. Neither script normalizes, rescales, or handles NaN or 65535, so the pickles are already model-ready. The script that produced `vin_2` and `vin_3` from the raw BMS uploads is not released.

The scripts load `./models/lstm.pth`, `net_model.pth`, `netx_model.pth`, and `./data/Name_list.xls`. No `Name_list.xls` is present in DTI.zip, GIS.zip, or QAS_1.zip through QAS_5.zip. Without the `.pth` files, reproducing `Test_.py` means retraining rather than merely running it. Open questions are the three-file distinction and column order, `vin_3` scaling, and trained-weight availability.

Paper p. 3 states: "The sampling intervals meet the minimum data quality requirement of 30 s." Fig. 2d says its horizontal axis represents sampled frames at 30-s intervals. This is a stated quality floor and plotted frame spacing, not measurable from the release because it has no timestamps.

Fig. 2c says the four digits of a fault-type number represent TR, EL, ISC, and EA, with 1 indicating a fault. The public release instead contains a single per-vehicle 0/1 label in `DTI/Labels.xls` and `QAS_5.zip/Labels.xls`; fault type was not released. The Discussion states "9 types of fault tags" (p. 8) against four named types, and the other five are not identified in the paper or release.

## Block meanings (settled 2026-10-04)

Sources: the paper's Methods ("the cell state is the sum of the pack state and the deviation state", with equivalent-circuit pack and deviation models solved by a strong-tracking Kalman filter); Supplementary Information Fig. S4 (the deviation model with dU_i, dOCV_i, dSOC_i and dR_i) and Fig. S5 ("intermediate variables such as voltage deviation and SOC deviation"); and `Function_.py`, whose `solvers()` computes `DUi = OCVi - OCV - I*DRi` and `Xi = soc + xi`. `solvers()` and `calculate_volt_modepi()` are defined but not called in the released scripts, and the script that produced the files is not released, so the block meanings rest on the paper, the Supplementary Information and these formulas. The values were checked on all 80 DTI and 393 QAS vehicles with `scripts/checks/cao_vin3_soc.py` (output in `reports/checks/cao_vin3_soc.txt` and `cao_vin3_soc_per_vehicle.csv`).

Fleet-wide (raw, rows with placeholder values left out): cell SOC = pack SOC + dSOC_i holds in all 393 QAS vehicles (99th-percentile error at most 0.0011) and in 74 of 80 DTI vehicles (the other six are records of 4 to 74 rows). DTI dSOC_i is zero, so DTI has no per-cell state of charge. The voltage deviations are nearly the same for every cell in a row: the code gives every cell the same resistance offset (`DRi = 3e-6`), so cells differ only through dSOC_i on a flat OCV curve. The cell-to-cell spread of dU_i is zero in DTI and has a fleet median of 0.0003 V in QAS. In QAS its per-cell part does not follow each cell's distance from the row mean (median correlation 0.04) and follows the per-cell part of dSOC_i loosely (correlation 0.003 to 0.957 across vehicles, median 0.29). The paper's Eq. 3 defines the voltage deviation as each cell's voltage minus the Eq. 2 pack voltage, but the released block is not that difference (median correlation 0.04): it is the filtered deviation-model output of Supplementary Note S1, and it cannot be recomputed without the authors' unreleased OCV coefficients `b` (an approximate regeneration with a fitted OCV curve recovers the pattern across cells, median within-row correlation 0.885 in QAS, but not the size). Release quality (`scripts/checks/cao_release_quality.py`, raw values): the release does not appear fully cleaned as Supplementary Note S5 describes. QAS holds 386,281 placeholder codes in 352 of 393 vehicles, 1,410,385 rows with current at or beyond -1,000 A in the same 352 vehicles, and 433 zero cell voltages in 335 rows of 5 vehicles; DTI has 4 zero cell voltages in one row of one vehicle and nothing else. vin_2's model-output columns (the two leading columns and dU_i) hold numerical blow-ups of the authors' filter, values beyond 1e6 down to -5.09e12, in 546 rows of 90 QAS vehicles (9,061 values; none in vin_3, none in a first row). In QAS vehicle 189 the dU_i minimum (-69.44) is in row 7, a filter transient inside the near -1,000 A stretch that starts at row 0. `clean=True` masks all of these, including QAS current at or below -900 A (see below). Each vehicle's cell voltages may include median-filled values (Note S5), and the release does not mark them. The leading columns, vin_1 and the DTI extra column were tested with `scripts/checks/cao_regenerate.py` (output in `reports/checks/cao_regenerate.txt` and `cao_regenerate_per_vehicle.csv`). With `names="inferred"` the state-of-charge columns (`soc_pct`, `cell_soc_###`, `cell_dsoc_###`) are returned in percent for both brands: QAS values are multiplied by 100 (placeholder codes are left as released), DTI values are already percent. This is the only value the loader changes, and it is a unit conversion. `names="generic"` returns every value as released.

Labels (fixed 2026-10-03). Each brand has at most one `Labels.xls`, and it covers every vehicle of that brand whichever archive the vehicle sits in. The QAS sheet is only in `QAS_5.zip` but labels all 393 QAS vehicles across `QAS_1.zip` to `QAS_5.zip`. `systems()` now reads each brand's sheet once and applies it to all that brand's archives. Before the fix it applied a sheet only to vehicles in the same zip, so 311 QAS vehicles showed no label. With the fix: DTI 77 label 0 and 3 label 1, QAS 335 label 0 and 58 label 1, GIS 41 with no label (no sheet), 412 label 0 and 61 label 1 in all. `tests/test_loaders.py::test_cao_labels_apply_across_a_brands_archives` covers it.

The Zenodo description and paper state 515 vehicles, while the released archives contain 514 vehicle folders: 80 DTI, 393 QAS, and 41 GIS. The one-vehicle difference is an open question.

Measured with vehicle `0` from each brand through the released archive: DTI `vin_1` is `(74, 1, 7)`, `vin_2` is `(74, 175)`, and `vin_3` is `(74, 176)`; QAS `vin_1` is `(43546, 1, 7)`, `vin_2` is `(43546, 225)`, and `vin_3` is `(43546, 226)`; GIS `vin_1` is `(5168, 1, 7)`, `vin_2` is `(5168, 90)`, and `vin_3` is `(5168, 91)`.

## Loader cleaning option

`load(..., clean=True)` is an opt-in, value-wise mask. It replaces only values that cannot be measurements with `NaN`, never an entire row. The exact sentinel values measured in the release are `-1004.8`, `-1000`, `-999`, and `65535`. It also masks any numeric value with magnitude above `1e6`; inferred DTI and QAS cell voltages at or below zero; inferred temperatures below -40 degC or above 150 degC; inferred current values whose magnitude exceeds 1000 A; and, for QAS, inferred current at or below -900 A. QAS records stretches of current near -1,000 A (for example -995.0, -998.4) where no current was recorded: 1,775,068 of 14,319,036 QAS rows (12.4 percent) in 352 of 393 vehicles, raw data. No QAS current falls between -900 and -200 A, and the lowest current above -900 A in any QAS vehicle is -86.7 A, so the threshold separates the placeholder stretch from real charging. With inferred names, `clean=True` adds a boolean column `current_invalid`, True in rows whose current was masked. The authors' model outputs in those rows (the pack-model columns, dU_i, and in vin_3 the cell SOC and dSOC_i) were computed from the bad current and are suspect, so analyses that use them should leave those rows out.

No lower or upper plausible-operating-voltage bounds are applied. Low cell voltage, high temperature, and large current can be the observed fault signal. The returned frame records `masked_value_count` and `clean` in `attrs`. Raw values remain the default return value.
