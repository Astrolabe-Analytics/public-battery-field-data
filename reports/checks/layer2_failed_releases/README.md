# Layer 2 failed-release repair

The runner processes only previously failed or blocked releases, with no-build and smaller-copy work first. Held checksums are SHA-256 values computed from already-present files and recorded in `reports/verify_log.csv`; no source file is downloaded or changed. Timestamp rows that cannot be assigned a calendar month are preserved in an `undated` partition and counted in their manifests. The progress table records each release result and SSD space at the start and final checkpoint.

Finished on 2026-10-21: done 9 of 10 releases. The seven repair-built copies are now verified against the current code, with all 39 build-unit manifests current and none stale. The repairs cover lossless storage of mixed Birds CubeSat values, missing held checksums for Bilfinger 2025 and TUM FTM, preservation of invalid KU Leuven and Tongji timestamps in undated partitions, bounded M5BAT batches, and the Zhou loader-to-copy comparison. No data were downloaded or cleaned.

Held source sizes and checksums match their references in `source_verification.csv`. Tongji's converted loader inputs also match their committed conversion sizes and SHA-256 checksums in `converted_source_verification.csv`. Every output part passed hash and row-count checks. Loader values matched at fixed sample positions in every build unit, recorded in `loader_spot_checks.csv`. Full loader row counts match for the streamed releases in `loader_row_counts.csv`, using the complete projected M5BAT stream and the TUM/Zhou loader inventories. The other releases' full loader row counts were checked with their spot comparisons.

Cell to Pack and RISE ConBat remain no-build releases for the reasons in `progress.csv`. Changan's fallback and sensor-token fix are committed, but its full copy remains paused at 23 of 300 vehicles and is not verified. Partial output stays on the SSD only. The earlier verified releases are unchanged and were not revalidated under the new fingerprint.

Reproduce with `python scripts/checks/layer2_failed_releases.py`, followed by `python scripts/checks/layer2_code_fingerprint_check.py`. The default excludes Changan, and explicitly selecting it raises an error before reading or writing any data. Complete unit manifests are checked and reused on restart only after fresh source verification. The fingerprint check fails on missing or stale manifests and saves `code_fingerprint_check.csv`.
